Privacy Policy — Horokeep

Effective date: September 26, 2026

Horokeep is an app for cataloguing a watch collection and keeping its service records. It exists for iPhone and for Android, and this policy covers both. In short: your collection stays on your device. On Android nothing at all leaves it; on iPhone the only thing sent to a server is what is needed to sign you in.

The two versions differ, so read the one you are using. The Android app has no accounts and no sign-in, and does not hold the Android permission that would let it use the internet at all. The iPhone app has optional accounts, and signing in sends an email address. Where a section below applies to only one of them, it says so.

What stays on your device

On both platforms, everything you record about your watches is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to us or to anyone else:

  • Watch details — brand, model, reference, serial number, calibre, box and papers status
  • Acquisition details — purchase date, price, and source
  • Condition notes
  • Service history — dates, who carried out the work, cost, description, and any documents you attach
  • Wear log entries
  • Insurance fields — insurer, policy number, estimated value, renewal date (iPhone only)
  • Photographs you add, and their captions
  • Archive records for watches you no longer own, including the reason and date

There is no cloud sync in this version. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

On Android, there is also no backup. The app opts out of Android's automatic backup service, so your collection is not copied to Google Drive or anywhere else. That keeps it private, and it has a cost you should know before you build a long record: uninstalling Horokeep, or moving to a new phone, removes everything. The app says so at the foot of your collection.

What is sent off your device — Android

Nothing. The Android app requests no permissions, including no internet permission, so it has no means of transmitting anything anywhere. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

What is sent off your device — iPhone

Signing in requires a server, so account information does leave your device. This section applies to the iPhone app only; there is no sign-in on Android.

  • Your email address, and your display name if you provide one.
  • Your password, sent securely to create and verify your account. It is handled by our authentication provider and is never visible to us.
  • If you choose Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, that provider confirms your identity to us and shares the email address associated with the account. Apple's Hide My Email is supported — if you use it, we only ever see the relay address.

Authentication is provided by Firebase Authentication, a service of Google LLC. Their handling of that data is covered by Firebase's privacy documentation and Google's Privacy Policy.

What Horokeep does not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking. There is no analytics SDK in the app.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No crash-reporting service.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, so the app does not need to ask for tracking permission.

Photographs

Photographs are never uploaded anywhere, on either platform.

On iPhone, Horokeep asks iOS for access to the photos you choose, and records a reference to each one along with any caption you write.

On Android, photographs are added through the system photo picker, which hands the app only the images you pick — which is why Horokeep never asks for access to your photo library. A copy of each picked image is written into the app's own private storage so that it survives the original being moved or deleted. Those copies are removed when you remove the photograph, and when you uninstall the app.

Deleting your data

On Android, uninstalling the app removes your entire collection, its records and its photographs from the phone. There is nothing held anywhere else, and so nothing to ask us to delete. You can also delete individual watches, service records, wear entries and photographs from inside the app at any time.

On iPhone:

  • Your account, from inside the app: open the account screen (the person icon, top left) and choose Delete account. That permanently deletes your account and the email address held for it, and erases every watch, service record, wear entry and archive record stored on this iPhone. It cannot be undone.
  • Your collection: deleting the app from your iPhone also removes all locally stored watch, service, wear, and archive data.
  • If you would rather we did it: email lamine.ndy+support@gmail.com from the address you signed up with.

Children

Horokeep is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Material changes to what leaves your device will be described here rather than made quietly.
