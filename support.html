Support — Horokeep

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Horokeep keeps a proper record of a watch collection — what you own, when each piece was serviced, and when it was last worn.

Contact

Email lamine.ndy+support@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and its iOS or Android version — it makes most problems much faster to identify.

Frequently asked

Is my collection backed up or synced?

No, on either platform. Your collection is stored on that one phone. Deleting the app deletes that data, so treat the app as the single copy for now. Cloud sync is planned but is deliberately not enabled yet rather than half-built.

On Android this is worth reading twice, because it also means no Google Drive backup: moving to a new phone does not bring your collection with it. Nothing is uploaded, which is the point, and the cost is that the record lives in exactly one place.

Does the Android app need an account?

No. There is no sign-in on Android at all — open it and start cataloguing. It also holds no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

Why does the iPhone app have an account if the data is local?

So that your collection can follow you to a new device once sync arrives, and so the record is tied to you rather than to one phone. The account holds only your email address — see the privacy policy. This applies to the iPhone app only.

How do I delete my data?

Android: uninstall the app. That removes the collection, its records and its photographs; nothing is held anywhere else.

iPhone: open the account screen and choose Delete account, which deletes the account and erases the local collection. Or email lamine.ndy+support@gmail.com from the address you signed up with.

What is the Atelier tab?

Every watch ordered by what needs attention — overdue for service first, then due soon, then in good order, then anything never serviced. Service dates are the ones you enter; Horokeep records intervals rather than assuming them for you.

What does "At rest since…" mean in the Wear Log?

It is a note, not a nudge. If a watch has not been worn for a while, the app says so plainly so you can decide whether that matters. There are no streaks and nothing to keep up.

A watch I no longer own — do I have to delete it?

No, and it is better not to. Use Remove from Collection and choose a reason. The watch moves to the Archive with its full record — photographs, service history, and wear log — kept intact. Deleting permanently is a separate action, tucked away, and it cannot be undone.

Can I export my service history?

On iPhone, yes: open a watch, go to its Service History, and use the export button to produce a PDF — useful when selling a watch or for an insurer.

On Android, not yet. The record is kept, and export is the first thing planned after launch.

The app is in dark mode only. Will there be a light theme?

No. Horokeep is designed as a dark display case, and a light variant would be a different app rather than a setting.

Reporting a bug

Email the address above with what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps. If the app closed unexpectedly, saying roughly when it happened is enough — there is no crash-reporting service collecting that automatically.
